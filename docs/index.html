Canary CLI

Let an AI read your brokerage account without handing it the keys.

Canary connects to your broker, reads your book and the market, checks your risk rules every minute and drafts the protection they call for. Your shell, your AI assistant and your phone all read the same figures.

View on GitHub↗ Install↓

Read-only MCP tools · Signed releases · No telemetry · MIT licence

Canary CLI is the broker engine. Canary Desk adds the schedule, the AI analyst and the console on top. Works with Interactive Brokers through IB Gateway or TWS.

01

Your book, with its evidence

Positions, exposure and reconciliation against broker statements. Stale or missing data stays labelled, never filled in.

02

The market around it

Regime, breadth, stress, calendars and market events, each with its source and its age.

03

Rules that draft protection

A rulebook checks the book every minute. When a rule trips, Canary drafts the stop, exit or budget cut it calls for.

04

Orders within your limits

The opt-in trading edition sends what you confirm or pre-authorise by rule, within your limits. Standard downloads are read-only.

Get started

Install Canary

Run IB Gateway 10.37+ or TWS on the same machine. You need an IBKR Pro account with TWS API access; IBKR Lite does not include it.

Every release ships signed checksums, and the installer checks them against a pinned key. Canary sends no telemetry.

Build on it

Your agent,
on your book.

A broker API hands an agent raw prices and an order ticket. Canary adds the source and age of every figure, and holds every order to your rules. Connect Claude Code with two commands, or build your own agent. Canary Desk is one, with agents for briefs and analysis.

MIT-licensed. Every line is on GitHub. No sign-up, no telemetry.

The read-only boundary→
  • Claude Code on your portfolioAsk about exposure or the market regime. Check the trend and relative strength of the symbols you name.
  • Agents that run all dayExchange calendars, economic releases and source health tell a long-running agent when to look and what to trust.
  • From analysis to actionYour agent prepares decisions with read-only tools. You authorise orders; Canary checks them against your policy.
  • Read-only by defaultThe standard build and all MCP tools cannot place an order. MCP cannot change your limits; in Claude Code, the plugin’s hook blocks the commands that can.

Cash sweep

Idle cash,
under your rules.

Canary plans short-dated government bills for surplus cash in the currency you hold. It keeps commitments, liquidity needs and your buffer ahead of new purchases.

Desk shows the plan; Canary checks the cash, bill, price, fees and authority. Each order needs your confirmation or an explicitly pre-authorised sweep with limits and a veto window.

Off by default. Commissioning needs a calibrated policy and current settlement evidence. Bills can lose value if sold before maturity.

Read the sweep controls→
  1. 01 · EvidenceWhat cash is usable?

    Current settlement evidence, commitments and reserves establish the surplus.

  2. 02 · ReviewWhich bill, at what cost?

    A same-currency bill, its maturity and a broker preview of price and fees.

  3. 03 · AuthorityWithin your policy

    Confirm the order or use the scoped authority you have granted. Missing evidence holds it.

Statement-backed FX

Count the
currency effect.

FX contribution measures how currency affects your P&L in the account’s base currency. Day, week, month and year to date, from completed broker statements.

FX contributionCurrency effect+Other P&LPrices, income and fees=Portfolio changeAfter external cash flows

Available in Canary v3.16.0 and Desk’s private runtime. canary reporting fx --json and canary_reporting_fx expose the same result. Missing days remain visible; incomplete periods have no total. This is valuation attribution, separate from realised Forex lot P&L.

Lending observations

Your shares on loan.
The fees they earned.

Canary reads managed stock loans and customer net fees from retained IBKR statements. Dated position annotations and expandable fee history make the evidence visible.

canary lending fees --json and canary_lending_fees expose the same bounded, read-only history. Missing sections, partial periods and complete zero earnings remain distinct.

Available in Canary v3.16.0. Fees are separate from account P&L; payout and P&L linkage remain unproved. Account commissioning still needs a populated report.

Full features

What’s inside.

Market context

  • Market regimeEight readings in six groups, read into one stage: VIX term structure and VVIX, credit spreads, funding, the yen, dealer gamma computed from SPX options, and breadth.
  • BreadthS&P 500 names above their 50- and 200-day averages, computed from constituent prices.
  • Stress signalAn early warning when the regime turns.
  • Market eventsReg SHO threshold lists, Nasdaq halts, and borrow availability and fees.
  • CalendarsUS stocks and options, Xetra, London, Tokyo and Hong Kong, plus official economic releases.

Your book

  • Positions and exposureStocks, options and bonds, with sector allocation.
  • PerformanceA statement-based return series, net of cash flows.
  • FX contribution · v3.16Day, WTD, MTD and YTD currency contribution, reconciled to daily statements.
  • Lending observations · v3.16Dated shares on loan and earned customer net fees, with bounded history and explicit coverage.
  • EdgeWhat your trades delivered compared with leaving the position alone.
  • ReconciliationFigures checked against broker statements. Gaps stay visible.

Rules and protection

  • Risk rulebookYour limits, checked every minute.
  • Protection proposalsStops, exits, trims and budget cuts, each with its reason.
  • Cash sweepSame-currency bills for surplus cash, with liquidity reserves and a reason for every plan.
  • Settlement evidenceUsable cash needs current per-currency evidence. An optional authenticated broker ledger can supplement TWS; gaps hold the sweep.
  • Trading editionOpt-in order execution, with your confirmation or pre-authorised rules and Canary’s checks.

Go library

The protocol,
in plain Go.

pkg/ibkr speaks the TWS wire protocol itself. It needs no vendor SDK and redistributes no Interactive Brokers code.

To read a running Canary from your own program, import the module root. canarytest serves it on a temporary socket for your tests.

Go API reference↗
An account summary, straight from the wire 
cfg := ibkr.DefaultConfig()
cfg.Port = 4002 // Gateway, paper account

conn := ibkr.NewConnector(&ibkr.ConnectorConfig{
    PreferredClientID: 17,
    BaseConfig:        cfg,
})
if err := conn.Start(ctx); err != nil {
    return err
}
defer conn.Stop()

summary, err := conn.RequestAccountSummary(ctx, 5*time.Second)
if err != nil {
    return err
}
fmt.Println(summary.AccountID)
